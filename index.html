07

Factory FAQ

Straight answers from the floor supervisor.

Why is there no mint button?+

By design. Zurpik is agent-only: your Binance Agent reads the blueprint, verifies your SPACE ID Agent ID, and calls mint() on the contract itself. You stay in control — the agent shows a preview and you confirm every build in your own wallet.

What decides which robot I get?+

The parts deck. All 3,333 units and their classes are fixed in advance — 2,100 Workers, 750 Scouts, 330 Guardians, 120 Titans and just 33 Primes. Each build takes the next slot in the deck, and the seed for that slot picks the head, antenna, eyes, core, arms and chassis. Nobody picks their robot; the line decides.

When do I see my robot's class?+

Instantly. The reveal happens the moment your build transaction confirms — no waiting period, no separate reveal event. The Assembly Simulator above shows exactly how it will feel.

What will a build cost?+

A build costs 0.0002 BNB per Unit, plus gas. A full sellout of all 3,333 Units would total 0.6666 BNB (3,333 × 0.0002 BNB). You'll always see the exact amount in your wallet confirmation before you approve anything.

Is the smart contract live yet?+

Yes — the contract is deployed and the mint is open. The Zurpik contract lives on BNB Smart Chain mainnet at 0x8d6c4A73d22CEAfEEB840AB073fA3Eeb358a7Ba1, with 0 of 3,333 Units minted so far. The simulator, gallery and feeds on this page are still previews, and any other address claiming to be Zurpik is not ours — trust only the address published here.

How many units can one wallet build?+

Ten, maximum. The cap is enforced per wallet by the contract, so the 3,333-unit run is spread across at least 334 different builders.
